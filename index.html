Our Story

A Passion for Flavour, Born in 2018

SaffronBites was born from a simple belief — that Indian cuisine, at its finest, deserves a stage worthy of its complexity. Our head chef trained under Michelin-starred mentors in London before returning to Delhi to create something truly original.

We source our spices directly from small farms in Rajasthan, Kerala and Kashmir — ensuring that every dish carries an authenticity that no supermarket spice blend can replicate.

Farm-to-table ingredients from 12 partner farms
Award-winning wine & cocktail programme
Private dining rooms for up to 20 guests
